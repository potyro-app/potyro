## AI makes mistakes

Apps made in the Studio are written by an AI model. Answers inside apps, like the lines read off a
receipt, also come from an AI model. Both can be wrong, and an app can do something you did not
expect.

Check what matters before you rely on it. Do not rely on an app or an AI answer alone for health,
money, legal or safety decisions.

## The free AI allowance

People with no key of their own get a free allowance, paid by us.

- It has limits: per device per day and per month, per internet address per day, and a total for
  everyone each month. When it runs out, you can add your own key.
- We can change the limits, pause the allowance, or end it.
- Use it for Potyro, through Potyro. Do not use it for anything else.

## Your own keys

from your device to that service, under its terms, and any cost is on your account there. We never

## Your data can be lost

Potyro is a young project. Keep your own copy of what matters to you: Export saves an app and its
records as a file.

- An app that is not synced or backed up exists only on your device. If the device or the browser's
  storage is lost, the app is lost.
- Synced and backed up apps are on one server. Backups of it are being set up. Until they are, a
  failure of that server could lose what is on it.
- If you lose your recovery words and all your devices, nobody can bring back your synced apps. We
  cannot read them, and we cannot recover your key.
