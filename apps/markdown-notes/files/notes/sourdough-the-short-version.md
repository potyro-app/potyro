# Sourdough, the short version

One loaf. Start the evening before you want to bake.

## Ingredients

- 100 g active starter (fed that morning, doubled)
- 375 g water, lukewarm
- 500 g bread flour
- 10 g salt

## Steps

1. Mix the starter into the water, then add the flour. Leave it 30 minutes.
2. Add the salt with a splash of water and squeeze it through.
3. Over the next 3 hours, **stretch and fold** four times, half an hour apart.
4. Shape it, flour a basket, put the loaf in seam up. Fridge overnight.
5. Morning: oven to 250 °C with the pot inside. Bake 20 minutes with the lid on, 25 without.
6. Wait an hour before cutting. *This is the hard part.*

## Notes to self

- Too flat last time: the starter was not ready. Wait for it to double.
- Scoring deeper gives a better ear.
