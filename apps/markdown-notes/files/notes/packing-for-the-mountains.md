# Packing for the mountains

Three days, two nights in the hut. Nights go down to about 5 °C.

## Wear or carry

- [x] Boots, broken in
- [x] Rain shell
- [ ] Fleece
- [ ] Hat and thin gloves
- [ ] Two pairs of socks, one dry

## In the pack

- [ ] Sleeping bag liner (the hut has blankets)
- [ ] Head torch, spare batteries
- [ ] Water bottle, 1.5 l
- [ ] Sunscreen and lip balm
- [ ] Map and the hut's phone number

## Food

- Oats and dried fruit for two breakfasts
- Bread, cheese, a cucumber
- Nuts and chocolate for the climbs
- *Dinner is at the hut: book it the day before.*

> Leave the extra camera lens at home. You never use it up there.
