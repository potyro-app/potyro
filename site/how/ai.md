## What an app can ask

Read a receipt into lines you can share out. Name a plant from a photo and say what might be wrong
with it. Turn a poster into an event in your calendar. Answer a question about what the app holds.
The app passes the photo or the text and its question; nothing else in the app goes with it.

## Asked first

An app someone else made asks once, on your phone, before its first call: which service, what
goes, and who pays as things are now. **Not now** refuses this call and asks again next time;
**Allow** is remembered for that app. You can turn AI off for any app, your own included.

## Who pays

Free first: a monthly allowance paid by Potyro, through a relay that holds Potyro's key and caps the
spending. Then your own key, which stays on your device; the app never sees it. With neither, the
call fails and says so in words.
