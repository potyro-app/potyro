## Three kinds of sharing

- **A link anyone can try.** The app, running, straight away: no install, no account, with the
  app's own sample records and nothing of yours.
- **Use it together.** The same app and the same records, live, with the people you invite.
  [Together, in real time](/how/together/).
- **A live session.** A room for the moment, for games and drawing together.

## What a demo link carries

The app's code, the files it ships with and its sample records, encrypted with a fresh key that
travels in the link and never reaches our server. The server keeps what a preview needs and you
chose to make public: a title, a line, a picture. Whoever opens the link sees the app in their own
language, in the safe box every app from someone else runs in.

## Make it mine

From a shared app, **Make it mine** copies it as a new app of your own, with credit to whoever made
it ("Remix of Groceries by Ana"), and opens it in the Studio, ready for your first change. **Keep
this app** keeps it as it is.
